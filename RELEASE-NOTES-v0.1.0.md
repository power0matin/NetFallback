  <h2>Integrity and safety</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>When an upstream release publishes an official SHA-256 digest, the downloader compares it with the downloaded file.</li>
    <li>A local hash alone is not independent publisher authentication or proof that a file is benign. Follow the upstream project's own signature-verification process.</li>
    <li>Public configuration snapshots and endpoints may expire or be unsuitable; review them before use.</li>
    <li>Downloaded programs are not automatically installed or executed.</li>
    <li>Never include credentials, private keys, or confidential configurations in a public release.</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong>Limitations:</strong> A successful download does not prove that a method works on a particular ISP or destination. NetFallback is a downloader and archive tool, not a VPN service or a guarantee of connectivity during a complete network shutdown.</p>
